## Overview

Workflow Agent is Applied Robotics Lab's flagship product — a local AI assistant that can control your computer to get things done. Instead of writing scripts or clicking through repetitive workflows, you describe what you want in plain language.

## How It Works

1. **Describe your task** — Type or speak what you want done.
2. **Agent plans** — Workflow Agent breaks it into steps.
3. **Executes locally** — It controls your apps, browser, and files.
4. **Save & reuse** — Store workflows for later or share with your team.

## Key Features

- Chat interface for natural language task execution
- Workflow library with save, edit, and share capabilities
- Desktop automation engine (clicks, typing, app control)
- Browser automation (web scraping, form fills, navigation)
- File processing (batch rename, move, convert)
- Managed AI models — optimized, updated by ARL

## Supported Platforms

- **Windows** 10 / 11 (x64)
- **macOS** 13 Ventura and later
