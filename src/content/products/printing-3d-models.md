## Overview

Applied Robotics Lab offers professional 3D printing services tailored for robotics developers, researchers, and product teams. We combine state-of-the-art hardware with engineering expertise to deliver functional, precise components.

## Technologies

### FDM Printing
Fused Deposition Modeling for functional prototypes using engineering-grade thermoplastics. Ideal for structural parts, enclosures, and fixtures.

### Resin Printing
High-resolution resin printing for detailed components requiring tight tolerances and smooth surfaces.

## Materials

| Material | Best For |
|----------|----------|
| PLA | Concept models, low-stress parts |
| PETG | Functional parts, chemical resistance |
| ABS | Heat resistance, automotive parts |
| TPU | Flexible components, gaskets |
| Resin (Standard) | High detail, smooth finish |

## Process

1. Upload your file (STL, STEP, 3MF, OBJ)
2. Receive quote within 4 hours
3. Approve and we start printing
4. Delivery or pickup in 24–72 hours
